ABSTRACT
With the development of computer technology, a variety of machine learning models have been used to predict
the electrochemical performance of energy storage devices. In this study, the elemental analysis, industrial
analysis and structural composition, activation conditions, and current density of biomass are innovatively
selected as input conditions from the biomass raw material characteristics perspective and predict specific
capacitance based on the light gradient boosting machine (LightGBM) and deep neural network (DNN) algo-
rithm. Meanwhile, the prediction effects of the contrast capacitance under seven different input combinations are
compared. The results show that the combination prediction effect of retaining all features is the best, and the
industrial analysis and structural composition of biomass have a greater impact on the model than elemental
analysis, this conclusion is also verified by SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) value analysis, indicating that
they are essential for the model. It is also found that the Light GBM model has more advantages, with R
2
of 0.951,
mean absolute error (MAE) of 11.090, and Root-mean-square error (RMSE) of 14.756. This study builds a key
bridge between the basic composition information of biomass raw materials and the electrochemical perfor-
mance. Also, it helps to understand the related formation mechanism of biomass-derived carbon materials.
1. Introduction
With the increasing emphasis on renewable energy and the
increasing demand for addressing climate change, the demand for effi-
cient energy storage technologies is becoming more and more urgent.
Therefore, creating innovative and effective energy storage solutions has
emerged as a significant global challenge [ 1 ]. Among the spectrum of
energy storage devices, supercapacitors, as a new form of energy stor-
age, have become highly promising candidates. Their advantages lie in
their high power density, wide temperature range, and economical and
environmentally friendly characteristics [ 2 , 3 ]. The selection and per-
formance of electrode materials have a crucial impact on the overall
performance and cost of supercapacitors. As a key component of the
supercapacitor structure, it directly determines its key parameters such
as electrochemical performance, energy density, power density, and
cycle life [ 4 ]. Among many electrode materials, biomass-derived carbon
has been widely used in supercapacitors due to its high specific surface
area (SSA), low cost, high porosity, large-scale production, and
environmental friendliness [ 5 , 6 ].
Although supercapacitors have been particularly widely used, man-
ufacturers are still seeking higher energy-density supercapacitors to
meet the needs of transportation, energy, and aerospace. [ 7 ] The com-
plex composition and structure of biomass make the synthesized carbon
materials often exhibit inhomogeneity [ 8 , 9 ]. Moreover, biomass-
derived carbon materials have irregular pore structures and single-
pore channels, resulting in an increase in the diffusion distance and
resistance of ions in the electrolyte, which significantly reduces the rate
performance of assembling supercapacitors [ 10 ]. Therefore, various
methods have been tried to prepare high-performance porous carbon
materials, such as the molten salt method, activation method, hydro-
thermal carbonization method, and template method [ 11 – 12 ]. In many
experiments preparing porous carbon materials for supercapacitors, a
common method is to chemically or physically activate biochar [ 11 , 12 ].
Physical activation involves subjecting carbon materials to high-
temperature atmospheres (CO2, O2, etc.) to form pores [ 13 ]. Chemi-
cal activation, on the other hand, involves using acids or strong bases

due to the direct proportionality between energy density and capaci-

most of the existing studies use the intermediate measurement variables
(SSA, pore volume, etc.) that have indirect effects on the contrast
capacitance as the input conditions of the prediction model, and the
basic composition information of the biomass raw materials is the key
factor affecting the biochar. It is the source variable that affects the
specific capacitance of carbon-based supercapacitors. These parameters
are obtained by experimental measurement, which are easier to obtain
than intermediate variables (SSA, pore volume, etc.), and are more
operable and feasible for experimental verification.
In this study, from the perspective of the characteristics of biomass
raw materials, no intermediate variable (specific surface area, pore
volume of biochar) was selected, it only selected the elemental analysis,
industrial analysis, structural composition, activation conditions, and
current density of biomass raw materials as input conditions. Based on
the Light GBM and DNN algorithm, a prediction model for the specific
capacitance of carbon-based supercapacitors was established. At the
same time, the prediction effect of the contrast capacitance under the
input combination of 7 different characteristics is compared. Then,
through the SHAP value analysis, the characteristics that have a great
influence on the contrast capacitance are found, and the partial corre-
lation analysis of these characteristics is carried out to explore the
specific influence of the input characteristics on the contrast capaci-
tance. This method of predicting the specific capacitance from the
perspective of the characteristics of biomass raw materials can correlate
these parameters with the specific capacitance by establishing a math-
ematical model under closer practical application conditions, simplify
the process of estimating the performance of electrode materials, and is
expected to improve efficiency and reduce costs. Meanwhile, it also has
certain reference significance in predicting other performance parame-
ters of supercapacitors.

The data of this paper are derived from the experiments of different

low temperatures and anaerobic conditions. To ensure that the physical
and chemical properties of pyrolysis biochar prepared in the first step of
each literature are roughly the same, the selection of pyrolysis process
parameters is relatively concentrated. The activation process is the
process of converting carbon-containing substances into activated car-
bon by thermal decomposition in a controlled atmosphere, heated in a
furnace or microwave. In this study, the data collected were limited to

2.3. Pearson correlation coefficient analysis
For multi-input and single-output predictive models, it is imperative
to reprocess the dataset to mitigate its complexity and enhance the
interpretability of output data [ 21 ]. In this regard, analyzing correla-
tions is pivotal, with the Pearson Correlation Coefficient (PCC) emerging
as a potent tool. PCC is typically utilized to assess the degree of corre-
lation between variables, with larger absolute values indicating stronger
correlations [ 22 ]. Generally, when PCC exceeds 0.6, it signifies a robust
correlation between two variables, and the simultaneous occurrence of
such strong correlations may lead to model overfitting, necessitating
trade-offs during the training process [ 23 ]. By scrutinizing the numeri-
cal distributions between capacitance and other variables, one can
construct a correlation matrix, facilitating effective feature selection.
2.4. Model development
To further enhance the diversity and persuasion of the study, the tree
model LightGBM and the typical neural network model DNN, which
performed well in previous studies, are selected for the prediction of
specific capacitance, they are more suitable for our data set. By
comparing the prediction results of the two models, we can evaluate
their accuracy and stability more comprehensively, and provide more
powerful guidance for practical application and decision making.
As a machine learning model based on a gradient boosting frame-
work, LightGBM provides an efficient and reliable basis for model
training and prediction. The technology of histogram storage features is
introduced into the LightGBM model, which can efficiently process
large-scale data sets and reduce memory consumption and computing
time. The gradient unilateral sampling algorithm introduced makes only
the data with a larger gradient absolute value concerned when each
node is split, which reduces the computational complexity. The mutu-
ally exclusive feature binding algorithm is used to optimize the feature
space, reduce the risk of model over-fitting, and improve the general-
ization ability. In addition, LightGBM also adopts the Leaf-wise growth
strategy, and each time selects the leaf nodes that reduce the loss
function the most to split, which can build a deeper tree faster. The
comprehensive application of these principles enables LightGBM to
significantly improve the training speed and efficiency while
maintaining the accuracy of the model and becomes one of the preferred
algorithms for solving regression and classification problems.
The principle of the DNN model is to transfer the input data layer by
layer through the construction and training of the multi-layer percep-
tron, and finally generate the output. The process continues through
forward propagation until the output layer. In neural networks, the
activation function, loss function, and optimization algorithm are the
key components. In this paper, ReLU is used as the activation function,
the negative input value is set to zero, and the positive input value re-
mains unchanged. The gradient descent method is used to solve the
optimal value of the mean square error loss function, and the model is
optimized by minimizing the mean square error to make it fit the
training data more accurately. The commonly used optimization algo-
rithms are the stochastic gradient descent method, momentum method,
and Adam method. In this study, the Adam algorithm is selected, which
combines the momentum method and the adaptive learning rate and has
better convergence speed and robustness. Compared with traditional
machine learning methods, the DNN model can learn deeper feature
representations, thereby improving the abstraction ability and general-
ization performance of data [ 32 – 33 ].
2.5. Evaluation of the model
In this study, 195 data sets related to the specific capacitance of
supercapacitors are divided into two parts, of which 80 % is used as a
training set for model training, and the remaining 20 % is used as a test
set for evaluating the performance of the model. In the hyperparameter
adjustment stage, to ensure that the model has good generalization
ability, it can also show good performance in the face of invisible data,
not just on the training data, we use the cross-validation method to train
models. The purpose of this step is through cross-validation, the per-
formance of the model on different data sets can be evaluated more
comprehensively and objectively. MAE is used as a criterion for
searching optimal hyperparameters, which helps to determine the per-
formance of the model under various parameter combinations. The
optimal hyperparameters corresponding to the model are determined
according to MAE. The performance and generalization ability of the
model are further improved by repeatedly training the machine learning
model. Then, the test set data is used for test verification to ensure its
effectiveness on unknown data. Combined with R
2
and RMSE for
comprehensive evaluation. The comprehensive assessment of these
metrics provides a thorough evaluation of the predictive ability of the
model, offering a reliable reference for subsequent practical
applications.

3.2. Pearson correlation coefficient analysis
Pearson correlation coefficient (PCC) serves as a valuable tool in
uncovering trends and relationships among variables, shedding light on
patterns and correlations within the dataset. Fig. 2 shows the correlation
between the specific capacitance of the supercapacitor and the influ-
encing factors (biomass raw material characteristics, activation condi-
tions, and current density), and intuitively shows the influence of
biomass raw material characteristics, activation conditions, and current
density on the specific capacitance of the supercapacitor. It can be seen
from Fig. 2 that the Pearson correlation coefficient (PCC) between each
input variable is < 0.6, indicating that the linear relationship between
each variable is weak, which ensures that the input features are rela-
tively independent, so these variables can be used as the input of the
model at the same time. It can be seen from Fig. 3 that carbon ( p <
0.001), hydrogen ( p < 0.05), and oxygen ( p < 0.01) in the analysis of
biomass elements, volatiles (p < 0.001), and Ash (p < 0.001) in biomass
industrial analysis, and cellulose (p < 0.01) in biomass structure
composition are significantly correlated with specific capacitance,
indicating that the specific capacitance of biomass raw materials is a
very important influencing factor. At the same time, the activation
temperature (p < 0.001), heating rate (p < 0.001), and residence time
(p < 0.001) in the activation conditions are also significantly correlated
with the specific capacitance. As reported in the literature, the physical
and chemical properties of porous carbon will be significantly affected
by activation temperature, activation time, and heating rate [ 28 ],
indicating that the activation conditions of biomass are very important
factors affecting the specific capacitance; the current density in the test
conditions of the capacitance also has a significant effect on the capac-
itance ( p < 0.01). In summary, the raw material characteristics, acti-
vation conditions, and current density of biomass have been proven to
have a certain influence on the specific capacitance of supercapacitors.

3.5. SHAP value analysis

prediction, which characterizes the relative importance of each feature
for model prediction. Its positive or negative number indicates the
positive or negative impact of the feature on the prediction. By analyzing
the SHAP value, we can determine which features are more important
for the prediction of the model, select features, and evaluate the
importance of features. At the same time, it can also help readers un-
derstand and explain the prediction process of machine learning models,
which plays an important role in improving the interpretability and
credibility of the model [30]. In this study, SHAP value analysis was
performed on all features under Light GBM and DNN models, and the
results are shown in Fig. 4.
Fig. 4 (a) shows the order and importance of importance of all fea-
tures under the Light GBM model, and Fig. 4 (b) shows the order and
importance of importance of all features under the DNN model. From the

models, the characteristics with greater influence on the contrast
capacitance are almost the same, including current density (Cond),
residence time (RT), activation temperature (AT), Ash, volatile matter
(VM), lignin (Lig) and hemicellulose (Hem). Among them, current
density (Cond) and residence time (RT) are the most significant char-
acteristics affecting the specific capacitance in the two models. It can be
seen that the SHAP values of these two characteristics have coverage
points from negative to positive values, and the degree of accumulation
is smaller than the following characteristics. The explanation is the most
important feature that affects the model, and the influence instability
plays a different important role in different samples. At the same time, it
can be found that the combined effect of industrial analysis and struc-

et al. [31] showed that cellulose, hemicellulose, lignin, and Ash of
biomass had a significant impact on the properties of biomass-derived
carbon materials. Therefore, it is very important to deeply study the
specific influence of the composition of biomass raw materials on the
specific capacitance and other characteristics of carbon materials, which
helps us to understand the formation mechanism of biomass-derived
carbon materials.

gradually increases, and with the increase of lignin content, the specific
capacitance gradually decreases, because the hemicellulose-rich derived
carbon materials in biomass usually have higher surface area, richer
pore structure, and better electrochemical performance in super-

diversity of cellulose, hemicellulose, and lignin in biomass will directly

that the specific structure in the structural composition of biomass
covers more useful information and has certain advantages in the pre-
diction of specific capacitance. Therefore, it has great potential to
incorporate it into the specific capacitance prediction model.
4. Conclusion
In this study, the elemental analysis, industrial analysis, structural
composition, activation conditions, and current density of biomass raw
materials were innovatively selected as input conditions from the
biomass raw material characteristics perspective. Based on the Light
GBM and DNN models, the specific capacitance of biomass-derived
carbon materials supercapacitors was predicted. At the same time, the
prediction effect of the contrast capacitance under the input combina-
tion of 7 different characteristics is compared. Then through the SHAP
value analysis, the characteristics of the greater influence of the contrast
capacitance are found, and the factors that have a greater influence on
the contrast capacitance are deeply explored. The results show that:
(1) The combination of retaining all features covers multiple aspects
of biomass characteristics, which has the best prediction effect, and the
industrial analysis and structural composition of biomass have a greater
impact on the model than elemental analysis.
(2) The LightGBM model is more suitable for the prediction of spe-
cific capacitance, R

is 0.951, MAE is 11.090, RMSE is 14.756, and MAE
and RMSE are significantly lower than the existing reported prediction
results.
In summary, it is feasible to establish a capacitance prediction model
based on the characteristics of biomass raw materials. This model builds
a bridge between the basic components of biomass and electrochemical
performance. This study provides a new idea for optimizing the prepa-
ration of biochar-based energy storage materials and provides a theo-
retical and practical basis for the design of high-performance
supercapacitor materials. In the future, it is possible to compare more
different models to predict the performance of supercapacitors, try
ensemble learning methods to improve performance, and use larger data
sets. Explore multi-scale modeling methods, connect microstructure and
macroscopic properties, design and synthesize high-performance
biomass supercapacitor materials, and promote the sustainable use of
energy.
