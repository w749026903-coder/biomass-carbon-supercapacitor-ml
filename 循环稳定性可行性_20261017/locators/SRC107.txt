The GCD profiles of BLPCs are shown in Fig. 6a at a current density of 30 mA g⁻ 1 (1C = 300 mA g⁻1) in the voltage range of 1.5 – 0.005 V. The BLPC-800 delivers a superior reversible capacity of ~310 mAh g⁻1, with an excellent capacity retention of 87.4% after 50 cycles (Fig. 6b). On the other hand, BLPC-700 and BLPC-900 attain reversible capacities of ~265 and 295 mAh g⁻1, respectively, retaining specific capacities of about 84.2% and 88%, after 50 cycles. Moreover, BLPCs show excellent rate performance at varying C-rates ranging from 0.1C to 10C, as illustrated in Fig. 6c. The specific discharge capacities recorded for BLPC-800 were 246, 191, 155, 110, 62, and 24 mAh g⁻1 at the current rate of 0.2C, 0.5C, 1C, 2C, 5C and 10C, respectively. Interestingly, the specific discharge capacity recovered to 321 mAh g⁻¹ at 0.1C, or 103.5% of its initial value, after 35 cycles of the rate performance test. In addition, the repetitive cycling of BLPC-800 at 1C was performed (Fig. 6d) for 1000 long cycles, leading to the reversible capacity retention of 74% with remarkable coulombic efficiency >99 %. To gain insight into the charge storage phenomena, the differential capacity versus voltage profile was plotted.
