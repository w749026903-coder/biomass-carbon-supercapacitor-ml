The stability of the material was investigated Fig. 6(a) shows the comparison of the voltammograms of MC-S-3@NF in 3.0 M KOH solution at the 1st and the 2500th cycles, while Fig. 6(b) shows the plots of the oxidation peak currents of the voltam- mograms over 2500 cycles.
