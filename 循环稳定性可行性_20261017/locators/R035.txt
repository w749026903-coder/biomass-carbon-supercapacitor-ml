Conclusions A method for obtaining nanoporous carbon from WSs modiﬁed with NiO nanoparti- cles is proposed. It is shown that preliminary mechanical activation of carbonized material before the process of thermochemical activation contributes to a more uniform distribution of particle sizes of the obtained nanoporous carbon material. The preliminary mechanical activation on carbonized WSs in a high-energy 3D ball mill creates a highly developed porous structure in carbon materials with a speciﬁc surface area of 2200 (±50) m2/g.
