Second, structural analyses reveal that the enhanced defect density (I D / I G ratio of 0.92) creates favorable ion-storage sites, directly translating to remarkable electrochemical performance. The material demonstrates outstanding cyclic stability with 89.90% capacitance retention and 117.21% Coulombic efficiency over 10,000 cycles, alongside a notable specific capacitance of 232.92 F/g at 0.2 A/g in a three-electrode configuration. These findings establish a paradigm for defect-engineered carbon architectures derived from sustainable biomass precursors, offering a novel strategy for designing high-performance energy storage materials through the precise control of pore topology and defect chemistry
