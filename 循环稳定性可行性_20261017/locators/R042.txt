aterials Yu-Cheng Chang * , Pei-Lin Fu , Shao-Ru Chang Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Feng Chia University, Taichung, 40724, Taiwan ARTICLE INFO Keywords: Supercapacitor Coffee grounds Biomass carbon Ni(OH) 2 Microwave-assisted hydrothermal ABSTRACT This research investigated the electrochemical properties and cycling stability of coffee grounds-derived biomass carbon (CGBC) and Ni(OH) 2 -CGBC composite materials as supercapacitor electrodes. These materials were produced by transforming coffee grounds into biomass carbon through thermal annealing and then growing Ni (OH) 2 nanosheets onto the CGBC using a microwave-assisted hydrothermal method. Optimal properties were achieved with 10 mM Ni(OH) 2 precursors for enhancing electrochemical performance. Various analyses, including FESEM, XRD, FETEM, and XPS, confirmed the uniform incorporation and beneficial properties of Ni (OH) 2 -CGBC composite materials. Electrochemical tests under the 1 M KOH solution revealed significant im - provements in specific capacitance and coulombic efficiency, maintaining good reversibility across different current densities. After 1000 charge-discharge cycles at 1 A/g, the capacitance retention rates were 64.8 % (CGBC) and 55.9 % (Ni(OH) 2 -CGBC), with stable coulombic efficiencies of 97.5 % and 96.8 %, respectively. These results suggest that Ni(OH) 2 -CGBC composite materials hold significant promise for long-term super - capacitor applications. 1. To evaluate the cycle stability of the CGBC and Ni(OH) 2 -CGBC composite (10 mM Ni(OH) 2 precursors) materials prepared as electrode materials for supercapacitors, a 1000-cycle charge-discharge test was conducted.
