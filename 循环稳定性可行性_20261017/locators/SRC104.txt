When applied as a supercapacitor electrode, the HTWB -0.5-900 material, obtained at 900 °C using a Na₂S₂O₃/HTWB mass ratio of 0.5/1, delivers the highest specific capacitance of 175 F/g at 0.5 A/g in 1 M KOH. Furthermore, this electrode exhibits exceptional cycling stability, maintaining its initial capacitance after 10,000 charge -discharge cycles, even at a current density of 10 A/g. Overall, the findings highlight the potential of converting hookah tobacco waste into efficient and sustainable electrode materials for high-performance supercapacitors.
