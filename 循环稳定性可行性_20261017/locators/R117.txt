For realizing practical applications, it is necessary that the supercapacitor electrode material should be elec- trochemically stable over a number of cyclic studies. The stability of ACRS-700 and ACEW-700 for 8000 cycles has been evaluated at a current density of 10.0 A g /C0 1 and the cyclic GCD plots are given in Figure 7G. The batches ACRS-700 and ACEW-700 exhibit exceptionally high capacitance retention, which is attributable to the grad- ual activation of carbon electrode generating more active sites for charge storage.
