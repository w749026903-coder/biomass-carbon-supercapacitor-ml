### PDF第7页检索定位

The structural, chemical, thermal, and morphological characteristics of date-palm-pollen-derived activated carbon were characterized using various analytical techniques. X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns of activated carbon were recorded using a Malvern PANalytical X’Pert3 X-ray diffractometer with Cu Kα radiation (λ = 1.5406 Å) operating at 40 kV and 40 mA over a scanning range of 10°–80° with a step size of 0.02° and a scanning rate of 2° min −1. The crystalline structure and the extent of graphitization were inferred from the XRD patterns.
